## What We Do

We design, build, and fly **ultra-lightweight high-altitude platforms** to collect and publicly release high-quality atmospheric data.

Aerostratospheric focuses on aerospace-engineered platforms and stratospheric research through **Midwest Stratospheric Data Systems**. We build payloads in the United States, fly them under FAA Part 101 guidelines, and release the resulting atmospheric datasets openly and rapidly — typically within 48 hours of successful recovery.

**Operations base:** Casey, Illinois (Clark County)  
**Mailing:** 1906 S Banker St #1002, Effingham, IL 62401

## Flagship Platform: X2Griffon

**X2Griffon (X2 Griffin)** is our ~**1.01 lb** Midwest-made stratospheric platform carrying:

- Temperature, humidity, and pressure sensors
- GPS-based wind profiling
- Real-time **APRS** and **WSPR** telemetry
- Dual-camera stratospheric imaging systems

Complementary systems include **MAD-AP** mid-altitude drift platforms and a continuous ground weather station contributing to the **NASA GLOBE** program (registration **GO-4VW9B**).

Next public flight: **September 19, 2026** · Casey, Illinois

## Unified Open Global Weather (UOGW)

**The most comprehensive open multi-layer atmospheric dataset available.**

UOGW is Aerostratospheric’s open, versioned atmospheric data commons — curated with Midwest Stratospheric Data Systems, updated by independent daily GitHub Actions automations, and published for anyone to reuse.

**Coverage (as of August 2026):**
- **25+ datasets** across **6 layers**: Surface · Marine · Upper-air · Stratosphere · Satellite · Flight
- 2,931+ IGRA stations · 1,936+ NDBC stations · 132,500+ GHCN stations
- Continuous ground observations from Casey, IL
- First-party near-space flight products

## Core Principles

- **Open Data** — Public release with rapid turnaround. Attribution required; commercial use welcome under stated terms.
- **Midwest-made** — Payloads designed, assembled, and critical electronics built in-house in the United States for full control over quality and mass.
- **Safety First** — Insured operations, documented procedures, public telemetry, and respect for airspace and private property during recovery.
- **Lightweight** — Ultra-low mass systems simplify FAA Part 101 compliance, recovery logistics, and enable more frequent flights.
- **Transparent** — Full public telemetry and open science practices.

## Why It Matters

Vertical profiles of temperature, moisture, and wind remain sparse compared with satellite and surface networks. High-quality balloon data supports:

- Weather forecasting and severe-weather / tornado research
- Atmospheric process studies
- Environmental monitoring
- Education and citizen science
- Open science infrastructure

We release data for researchers, meteorologists, STEM educators, the amateur radio & HAB community, and citizen scientists across the Midwest and beyond.
